Across the six paired draws, removing Rasch produces lower mean ALC by $0.00003$ and lower ALC in 4 of the six seeds (Table~\ref{tab:rasch_removal}). Mean zero-label Brier changes from $0.2258$ to $0.2270$. The additional seeds use the same selected sources and frozen artifacts; they are a check of draw sensitivity, not a new-source generalization test. We adopt the simpler prior--kernel predictor, eliminating difficulty inference and ability updates. The measured change is small and includes a cold-start tradeoff; we do not claim uniform improvement across sources.

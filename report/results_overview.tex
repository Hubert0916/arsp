Table~\ref{tab:proxy} reports the \textbf{four predictor--acquisition combinations}, averaged equally over groups and then over seeds 42, 43, and 44. ASRP + Adaptive has a mean Brier ALC of $0.1987$, compared with $0.2435$ for Empirical Mean + Random (an absolute reduction of $0.0447$). Its aggregate Brier score is lower at 6 of the six budgets. The two predictors share identical labels within each acquisition policy; the Random and Adaptive policies use identical candidate orders and evaluation pools.

\paragraph{Cold-start transfer.} Frozen public priors, difficulty estimates, and topic corrections may transfer poorly before labels are available. Across 10 official evaluations, zero-label Brier is $0.2640$, above the $0.2500$ of a constant $0.5$ prediction. Revealed evidence reduces it to $0.1511$ at budget 31. The public ablations do not identify which initialization component causes the private-data errors; accurate cold-start prediction remains a limitation.

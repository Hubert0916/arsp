\paragraph{Cold-start transfer.} Without revealed labels, ASRP relies on frozen public-data priors, estimated item difficulty, and topic corrections. Across the 10 official evaluations, its mean zero-label Brier score is $0.2640$, compared with $0.2500$ for a constant prediction of $0.5$ on any binary outcome. This identifies a weakness in the combined initialization on the sampled private data; the aggregate scores do not identify which component is responsible. Revealed evidence reduces mean Brier to $0.1511$ at budget 31, but accurate prediction before acquiring labels remains a limitation.

\textbf{All three removals increase mean ALC, but their effects depend on the budget.} Removing topic calibration has the largest mean ALC increase ($+0.001007$); its Brier penalty is largest at budget zero ($+0.0062$), whereas removing it slightly improves the mean scores at budgets 7--31. Removing the kernel raises Brier score most at budget 31 ($+0.0040$), consistent with its role in using accumulated support. Removing Rasch changes mean ALC by only $+0.000091$: it worsens mean scores at budgets 0, 3, and 7, improves budget 1, and leaves budgets 15 and 31 unchanged because the ensemble uses only the kernel at those budgets.

\textbf{The average benefit is not uniform across draws.} ASRP has lower ALC than the no-Rasch, no-kernel, and no-topic variants in 17, 18, and 19 of 30 draws, respectively. These fixed-artifact removals identify budget-specific effects in this public snapshot; the small Rasch and kernel ALC differences do not establish a consistent improvement on unseen groups.

\paragraph{Rasch has budget-dependent effects.} Across six paired draws, Rasch lowers zero-label Brier from $0.22696$ to $0.22583$ in the aggregate, with this direction in all 6 seeds. It also slightly improves budget 7, but worsens budgets 1 and 3 (Table~\ref{tab:rasch_retention}). \textbf{Aggregate ALC hides these offsetting effects}: the budget-zero gain contributes $-0.000113$ to ALC, while budgets one and three contribute $+0.000065$ and $+0.000102$, respectively. The net ALC change is $+0.000031$. At budgets 15 and 31, $(1-w_n)\eta_n=0$, so this ablation cannot assess Rasch there. Effects also vary by source: at budget zero, Rasch improves 43 of 92 source means and worsens 49. The evidence supports an initialization contribution in the frozen configuration, rather than a uniform advantage throughout the few-label regime.

\paragraph{Why retain Rasch?} Across six matched draws, retaining Rasch lowers zero-label Brier from $0.22696$ to $0.22583$, with the same direction in all 6 seeds (Table~\ref{tab:rasch_retention}). Overall ALC is essentially tied: $0.19875$ with Rasch versus $0.19872$ without it; retention improves ALC in only 2 of six seeds. Budgets 15 and 31 are identical because the hybrid has zero weight. \textbf{The controlled evidence supports a small initialization benefit, not an aggregate ALC advantage.} We retain the original limited-weight Rasch term to preserve this cold-start contribution; the less favorable subsequent no-Rasch official batch adds a practical reason to withdraw the simplification (Section~\ref{sec:official}). All six draws reuse the same sources, and the official batches are unpaired, so neither establishes a general transfer benefit.

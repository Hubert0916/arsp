\textbf{The evaluation separates prediction quality from label selection.} Across 30 complete-group public draws, ASRP achieves mean Brier ALC $0.1907$ versus $0.2294$ for Empirical Mean under identical Adaptive evidence. Switching ASRP from Random to Adaptive acquisition increases mean ALC by $0.0013$. Component ablations find that Rasch has mixed group-dependent effects, while kernel adaptation and topic calibration yield small mean improvements under Adaptive evidence.

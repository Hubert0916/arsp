\textbf{Matched randomly acquired labels isolate the predictor comparison.} Across 30 complete-group public draws, ASRP achieves mean Brier ALC $0.1895$ versus $0.2285$ for Empirical Mean. All six mean budget scores favor ASRP. Component removals identify the largest topic-calibration effect at budget zero and kernel effect at budget 31, while the mean Rasch effect is small. These findings concern predictor changes with label selection held fixed; public fitting and evaluation overlap, so cold-start transfer to unseen benchmarks remains an open question.

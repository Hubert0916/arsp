\paragraph{Kernel adaptation contributes at higher budgets.} Removing the kernel branch increases ALC from $0.1987$ to $0.2012$, the largest increase among the three removals ($0.0025$). At budget 31, Brier worsens from $0.1738$ to $0.1829$. This supports the kernel branch at higher budgets; its zero weight leaves budgets zero and one unchanged.

\paragraph{Calibration and Rasch have smaller, different effects.} Removing topic calibration increases ALC to $0.1995$ ($+0.0008$); its largest Brier benefit is at budget zero. Removing Rasch slightly lowers ALC to $0.1985$ ($-0.0002$), although zero-label Brier worsens from $0.2263$ to $0.2277$. The small ALC change varies in sign across seeds. Rasch helps initialization but not mean ALC here. Metadata smoothing and the kernel baseline remain, so the broader improvement over Empirical Mean cannot be assigned to Rasch alone.

\paragraph{Controlled component effects.} Removing Kernel Probe and topic calibration increases mean ALC by $0.00047$ and $0.00075$, respectively. Rasch removal changes mean ALC by $-0.00024$; a paired-draw 95\% bootstrap interval is $[-0.00256, 0.00202]$. This interval spans zero and establishes neither an average advantage nor equivalence. Zero Rasch weight makes predictions identical at budgets 15 and 31. See Appendix~\ref{app:rasch_audit} for resampling and diagnostic checks.

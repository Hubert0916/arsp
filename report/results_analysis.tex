\paragraph{Matched-evidence comparison.} With randomly acquired evidence held fixed, ASRP changes ALC by $-0.0391$ relative to Empirical Mean and scores lower in 28 of 30 draws. The comparison measures the complete predictor under the same label-selection rule. The largest mean Brier reduction is at budget 1 ($0.1407$), where Empirical Mean predicts only zero or one from a single label. ASRP instead combines its prior with the observed response. The gap narrows as more labels stabilize the empirical estimate.
